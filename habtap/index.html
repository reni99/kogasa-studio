HabTap

Habit tracker

Track a habit with a single tap. A calm grid of your habits, a year of progress at a glance, and no pressure to be perfect: missing a day now and then matters far less than showing up over time.

iPhone · iPad · Apple Watch · Free

Download on theApp Store

What it does

Small steps, kept simply

HabTap does one thing: it helps you notice the small things you do every day, and how they add up over a year.

  • One tap to track

    Tap a habit to mark it done. Habits with steps, like six glasses of water, take one tap per step. Tap again to undo.

  • Goals that fit

    Not every habit is a daily one. Set a goal for every day, or a number of times per week or per month.

  • A year at a glance

    Each habit has its own year overview and calendar. Forgot to log yesterday? Fill in any past day.

  • Statistics

    Completion rates and logged habits for the year, per habit and overall. Share a picture of your year if you want to.

  • Gentle reminders

    Optional reminders on the days and at the times you choose, for all habits or for a single one. Off until you turn them on.

  • Everywhere you are

    Widgets for your Home Screen and an Apple Watch app. Your habits sync through your own iCloud, with no account to create.

Privacy policy

Your habits are yours

We do not collect any data in HabTap. Your habits live on your devices and in your own iCloud account, where only you can read them.

  • No account
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No ads, no third-party code
  • Nothing sold or shared

Who we are

HabTap is made by Kogasa Studio, an independent app studio in Switzerland. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use HabTap on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and in its widgets. Who is responsible is in our legal notice. If you have a question, write to us at hello@kogasastudio.com.

What HabTap stores

To work, HabTap keeps the following on your device:

  • your habits: name, icon, colour, goal and steps per day
  • the days and steps you logged for each habit
  • your reminder times and app settings, such as the appearance

This data is shared between the HabTap app, its widgets and its Apple Watch app on your devices. It is never sent to us.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, HabTap keeps your habits in sync between your devices using Apple’s CloudKit. Your data is stored in the private database of your own iCloud account. Only you can read it: we, as the developer, have no access to it. Apple encrypts it in transit and in storage, as described in Apple’s iCloud security overview, and Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud.

If iCloud is turned off for HabTap, your data stays on the device only. To keep devices in sync, Apple may send HabTap silent notifications when your data changes on another device; these come from iCloud and carry nothing from us.

Reminders

Reminders are optional and off until you turn them on. They are scheduled on your device by iOS and watchOS. No server of ours is involved.

Sharing your statistics

You can create a picture of your year and share it. The picture is made on your device and only goes where you send it, through the system share sheet.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting services, and HabTap includes no third-party code that does.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any information about you.

Apple may ask you to rate HabTap with its standard rating prompt; that is handled by Apple. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics such as the number of downloads or crashes. These never identify you.

Deleting your data

Deleting a habit in HabTap removes it from your devices and your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on that device. To remove the copy in iCloud as well, open Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › HabTap and delete its data.

Writing to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Children

HabTap collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to know what personal data is held about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Because we do not hold any data about you from HabTap, there is nothing for us to hand over or delete; the data in your iCloud is under your control. You are welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes

If HabTap ever changes how it handles your data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
